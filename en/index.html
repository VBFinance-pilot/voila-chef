The concept

A private dining house born in Kyoto, where French cuisine meets the Japanese terroir.

Voilà chef brings Kazu and Yuichiro together around one standard: exceptional produce, a short and certain list, and a taste for the occasions that matter.

Every table is composed to measure — for a dinner among friends, a family celebration, a wedding or a corporate retreat. French cuisine, Japanese cuisine, or both at the same table.

The chefs

Two cuisines, one standard.

We would rather show you their plates than their faces.

French cuisine

Kazu

Trained in the French school — stocks, sauces, slow cooking, pastry — Kazu serves it with Japanese precision and restraint. The classics, without ostentation; the right gesture, on every plate.

Japanese cuisine

Yuichiro

Yuichiro cooks Japan in its season: fish from the market, Kyoto vegetables, dashi, fermentations. A cuisine of the essential, made for the table and for sharing rather than for the counter.
